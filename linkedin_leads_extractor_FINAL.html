⚡ How this works now — no clipboard limits

The bookmarklet saves all leads directly to a file as it goes. When done (or when you stop), it shows a Download JSON button on the Sales Navigator page. Click it to save the file, then drag it into the extractor in Step 2. No clipboard, no paste errors, works for 2000+ leads.


  1. Drag the blue button below into your browser bookmarks bar
  2. Go to page 1 of your Sales Navigator saved search (logged in)
  3. Click the bookmark — status box appears with Stop / Restart buttons
  4. It scrolls each page to force all 25 leads to render, then extracts and moves to next
  5. When done or stopped — click the Download JSON button that appears
  6. Come to Step 2 here and drag that JSON file into the drop zone

Resume: If you stop mid-way, the bookmarklet remembers where it left off (stored in LinkedIn's page memory). Click Stop, download what's collected, then click Resume to continue from the same page.

Restart: Click Restart to clear progress and start from page 1 again.

LinkedIn URLs: Converted to linkedin.com/in/MEMBER_ID — works after your Sales Nav subscription ends.
Email & phone: Not available on search results pages. Email and Phone columns are in the CSV for you to fill in manually or enrich with Apollo, Clay, or Hunter.io.
