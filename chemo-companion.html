🔍 Food Safety Check

Is this food safe during chemo?

✅ Always Safe

Dal-rice, khichdi, roti-sabzi, well-cooked vegetables, boiled curd/kadhi, steamed idli, moong dal chilla, upma, poha, vegetable soup, banana, pomegranate (peeled), papaya (peeled), turmeric milk, ghee, paneer (cooked), well-cooked eggs

🚫 Always Avoid

Raw salad, raw sprouts, uncooked curd/raita, street food, outside/restaurant food, leftover food, raw fruits with skin (grapes, berries), cut fruits from outside, cold drinks, ice cream from outside, raw/undercooked eggs, sushi, rare meat

📚 Guidance Sources
WHO — Food safety during immunosuppression WHO
• Cook all foods to safe temperature (>70°C)
• Use safe, clean water for cooking and drinking
• Avoid raw/undercooked animal products
• Wash hands before handling food
Oncology Dietitian Guidelines Clinical
• Neutropenic diet: no raw fruits/vegetables except those that can be peeled
• High-calorie, high-protein small meals 6x/day
• Avoid fermented/unpasteurized dairy during low WBC
• Probiotics only as doctor-approved supplements, not from food
• Ginger/lemon water for nausea management
