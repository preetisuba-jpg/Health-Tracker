🔍 Food Safety Check

Is this food safe during chemo?

✅ Always Safe

Dal-rice, khichdi, roti-sabzi, well-cooked vegetables, boiled curd/kadhi, steamed idli, moong dal chilla, upma, poha, vegetable soup, banana, pomegranate (peeled), papaya (peeled)

🚫 Always Avoid

Raw salad, raw sprouts, uncooked curd/raita, street food, outside/restaurant food, leftover food, raw fruits with skin (grapes, berries, apple with skin), cut fruits from outside, cold drinks, ice cream from outside
